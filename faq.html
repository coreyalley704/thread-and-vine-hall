On the day

What time do events end?

Saturday events end by [—], with load-out complete by [—], because our host church meets Sunday mornings. Friday events can run until [11:00 PM]. Sundays aren’t available for private events.

When can we get in to set up?

Setup time is part of your rental block. Saturday wedding rentals start at [12:00 PM]. Extra hours can be added in advance.

Can we have a DJ or live band?

Yes. Bring your own DJ or band. Amplified music ends by your event end time. The Loom has power and a stage ready for performers, and our technician runs the house system at [—]/hr.

What can we use to decorate?

Freestanding décor, florals, draping and string lights are welcome. No confetti, glitter, rice, birdseed, sparklers or open flame; LED candles are welcome, and real candles only in glass that fully encloses the flame, with our approval. Nothing nailed, taped, stapled or glued to the original brick or timber.

What happens if it rains on our deck plans?

The deck is a bonus, not a requirement. Anything planned for the deck moves inside to the Loom or the No. 6 Lounge, so there’s always a dry plan.

What do we clean up?

Take home your décor, gifts, food and any outside rentals, and bag your trash. Your [—] cleaning fee covers the rest: tables, chairs, floors, restrooms and linens. We walk the space with you before and after.
