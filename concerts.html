A music hall with mill walls.

Christian concerts and worship nights for up to 600, in a restored 1899 mill in the heart of Concord.

Why play here

Brick and old timber make a room sound warm. The Loom is big enough to hold a real crowd, and close enough that the back row can still see the singer’s face.

It fits the shows that are too big for a coffee shop and too small for an arena: touring Christian artists, worship nights, gospel and choir concerts, album releases and benefit shows.

Thread & Vine Hall is a ministry of SOJO Church, so the concerts we host feature Christian artists and music.

Who books shows here

Bring the music.

  • Christian artists and promoters looking for a mid-size room between Charlotte and the Triad
  • Worship nights for churches, campus ministries and worship teams
  • Gospel, choir and hymn concerts
  • Local Christian bands and songwriters hosting album releases and showcases
  • Benefit concerts for churches and partner nonprofits, at 50% off room rental
  • Christian school recitals and student showcases
