Who we are

Thread & Vine Hall is a ministry of SOJO Church. We open it to our city because good gatherings are a gift, and we want every renter to know how we work before they sign anything.

Every event follows the Facility Use Policy. We review the event, not the person, and we apply the same policy to everyone.
