Meetings worth showing up for.

Conferences, trainings, offsites and company parties in a restored 1899 mill in the heart of Concord.

Why here

Your team has sat in enough hotel ballrooms. Thread & Vine Hall gives them brick, timber and daylight instead, with the screens, sound and Wi-Fi a real workday needs.

Five rooms under one roof means a keynote, three breakouts and a coffee break can all happen without anyone leaving the building.

What companies host here

From a board meeting to a 600-person summit.

  • Conferences and summits. The Loom holds up to 600 with a stage, sound system and screens.
  • Trainings and workshops. The Spinning Room or the No. 6 Lounge, set classroom, rounds or U-shape.
  • Team offsites and retreats. A full day across two rooms, with coffee from the Bobbin.
  • Board and leadership meetings. The Thread Room seats 15 around one table.
  • Holiday parties and company celebrations. The Loom and the deck, with your caterer and bar.
  • Client events, mixers and product launches. The No. 6 Lounge for up to 50, or the Loom for more.
Up to 600 · The Loom

Conferences and keynotes

Rows or rounds facing the stage, with a podium, house sound and screens on both sides of the room. Breakouts fit in the Spinning Room and Thread Room.

Up to 50 · The No. 6 Lounge

Workshops and planning days

A U-shaped table, a whiteboard and a screen, with room to get up and move. Coffee is a few steps away.

Under 30 · The Bobbin

Breakfast meetings

One long table, a wall screen and breakfast on the bar. Good for client breakfasts and small-team check-ins.

Up to 600 · The Loom

Holiday parties and celebrations

Cocktail tables, a buffet, a bar and string lights between the columns. Bring your caterer, or walk next door to Gibson Mill’s restaurants and breweries.
