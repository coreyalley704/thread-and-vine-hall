Celebrations · Concord, NC

Throw the party they’ll talk about.

Birthdays, graduations, reunions and Christmas parties, in a restored 1899 mill with room for 10 to 600.

Check Availability
Your people, together

Some nights deserve more than a living room or a back room at a restaurant. Bring the whole crew to a place with brick walls, string lights and room to spread out.

You bring the cake and the playlist. We’ll have the tables set and the lights on when you walk in.

What we host

Every reason to gather.

  • Birthdays, from first birthdays to 50th and 80th milestones
  • Graduation parties for high school and college grads
  • Family and class reunions, with room for everyone
  • Christmas and holiday parties for families, neighborhoods and teams
  • Baby and bridal showers
  • Anniversaries and vow renewals
  • Retirement and going-away parties
  • Celebrations of life, held with care
Pick your room

Sized to your guest list.

Under 30 · The Bobbin

Birthdays and small parties

Café tables, a cake table and the coffee bar close by. Just right for a birthday, a shower or a graduation open house.

Up to 50 · The No. 6 Lounge

Christmas and holiday parties

Long family-style tables, a tree in the corner and warm light on the brick. Room for the whole family, kids included.

Up to 50 · The No. 6 Lounge

Showers, brunches and graduations

Long tables, flowers and a balloon garland if you want one. Bright, open and easy to decorate.

Up to 600 · The Loom

Reunions and big milestones

Room for the whole class or the whole family tree, with a buffet, a dance floor and string lights between the columns.

Open-air · The Deck

Dinner under the lights

A long table under the shade sail and string lights. Add it to a Loom or Lounge party for toasts and fresh air.

Good to know
  • Bring your own caterer, or order from Gibson Mill’s restaurants right outside the door.
  • Alcohol may be served free to guests by a licensed bartender, under our alcohol policy. Not at youth or kids’ parties.
  • Decorate your way with freestanding décor. No confetti, glitter or open flame (LED candles are welcome), and nothing attached to the brick or timber.
