Churches

A room for the Church to gather.

Worship nights, leadership retreats, conferences, and a Sunday home for new churches.

Talk to Us About Your Church Event
Better together

We believe the Church is better together. Whether you’re a church plant looking for a place to launch, a congregation planning a worship night, or a pastor gathering your team for a retreat, Thread & Vine Hall is ready to host you.

Orthodox Christian churches and ministries receive our 50% rate, with tables, chairs and linens included.

Ways churches use Thread & Vine Hall

Room for every kind of gathering.

  • Worship and prayer nights. The Loom holds up to 600, with a stage and sound system.
  • Conferences and equipping events. The Loom for main sessions, the Spinning Room and Thread Room for breakouts.
  • Staff and elder retreats. The Thread Room or Spinning Room, with coffee from the Bobbin.
  • Baptism celebrations, ordinations and special services.
  • Small groups, classes and youth gatherings. The Bobbin and the No. 6 Lounge.
  • Weddings and celebrations of life for your members. Standard wedding rates apply.
