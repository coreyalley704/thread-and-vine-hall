Nonprofits · 50% off

Your mission deserves a great room.

Churches, ministries and partner nonprofits get 50% off every space at Thread & Vine Hall.

Apply for the Nonprofit Rate
Why we do this

The best things in Concord happen because someone cared enough to start something. Food pantries. Mentoring programs. Schools. Recovery groups. Arts. Churches.

We want to be a place where that good work gathers, so we cut our rates in half for the organizations doing it.

What you get

The same room. Half the rate.

  • 50% off room rental in the Loom, No. 6 Lounge, Spinning Room, Bobbin and Thread Room
  • Our tables, chairs and linens, included
  • Wi-Fi and an on-site venue host
  • A flat [—] cleaning fee, and sound and AV at [—]/hr when you need it

Great for

Board meetings and retreats, volunteer trainings, fundraising dinners and galas, donor events, community classes, awareness nights, annual meetings.

Who qualifies
  • Orthodox Christian churches and ministries, affirming the Apostles’ and Nicene Creeds
  • Registered 501(c)(3) nonprofits that serve our city and whose mission and work line up with our Christian faith
  • The event serves your organization’s mission: fundraisers, meetings, trainings, community events
The fine print
  • The discount applies to room rental, not to add-ons, catering or bar service.
  • Saturday bookings in the Loom: [25% off instead of 50%, or 50% off when booked within 60 days of an open date].
  • [Up to 6 discounted bookings per organization per year.]
  • Private events for staff or members (weddings, birthdays) don’t qualify.
  • We’ll ask for your IRS determination letter or EIN and a certificate of event insurance.
  • Every event follows our Facility Use Policy.
