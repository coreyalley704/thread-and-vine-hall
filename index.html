The building

A century of thread.

Gibson Mill opened in 1899 [as Cannon Mills Plant No. 6 — confirm] and spun cotton into cloth for over a century. The looms are quiet now. The brick, the timber and the light are still here.

So every room carries a piece of that story, from the Loom where separate threads became one cloth, to the Bobbin that held the yarn.

The name

Why Thread & Vine.

Thread is what this mill made for more than a century: single strands, woven into something strong.

Vine is what grows when things stay connected to their roots, and keep bearing fruit.

Put them together and you have what happens here. People, plans and stories, woven together and growing.
