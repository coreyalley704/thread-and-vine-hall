Since 1899

A century of thread.

Gibson Mill spun cotton into cloth for over a hundred years. The looms are quiet now, but the brick, timber and light are still here, and every room is named for a part of the mill’s work.

Thread & Vine Hall is a ministry of SOJO Church.
